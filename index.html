02 — What it does


 


  

   01 — Lock degree sequence
   

    Each node's number of connections is recorded and preserved.
   

  


  

   02 — Rewire edges
   

    Pairs of edges are swapped without self-loops or duplicate
    edges, changing topology while preserving degree.
   

  


  

   03 — Compare structure
   

    The clustering statistic is measured across many rewired
    networks and compared with the observed value.
   

  


 





 
Open Full Evidence Registry






 
03 — What this is — and isn't


 

  This is a statistical network-analysis tool, not a proof engine.

  
    
   
  • 
    A result outside the null interval means the observed pattern is
    unusual relative to this particular degree-preserving null model.
   
    • 
   
  • 
    It does not establish causality or a physical law.
   
    • 
   
  • 
    Small networks and too few null samples can produce unstable results.
   
    • 
   
  • 
    Important findings should be independently replicated and checked
    by domain experts.
   
    • 
   
  • 
    This public version is intended for research, education and
    exploratory analysis.
   
    •

Evidence Governance

 
How E-ZERO Handles Claims


 

  E-ZERO separates software results, simulation, statistical evidence,
  and physical proof. Public limitations remain visible alongside claims.
 


 


  

   EVIDENCE-GOVERNED
   
Claims Follow Evidence

   

    Public capability and status wording must stay within the scope
    supported by the evidence registry.
   

  


  

   PASS + FAIL PRESERVED
   
Results Stay Visible

   

    Validation outcomes are preserved as recorded rather than silently
    rewriting or hiding unfavorable results.
   

  


  

   SIMULATION LABELED
   
Simulation Is Not Physical Proof

   

    Simulated, replayed, mocked, software-validated, and physical evidence
    are kept distinct.
   

  


  

   LIMITATIONS PUBLIC
   
Boundaries Stay Visible

   

    Diagnostic, control, hardware, safety, privacy, and field-validation
    limits are shown where relevant.
   

  


 


 

  EVIDENCE-GOVERNED · LIMITATIONS PUBLIC · PASS & FAIL PRESERVED ·
  SIMULATION LABELED · SOFTWARE EVIDENCE ≠ PHYSICAL PROOF
